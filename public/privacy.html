Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are paramount at FITUP. This Privacy Policy details the information we collect, how it is used, our rigorous security protocols, and your data rights across our Android and Web applications.

1 About FITUP & Scope

FITUP ("we", "our", or "us") operates the FITUP digital platform, accessible via Android mobile application and web browsers at https://fitup-ccb95.web.app. FITUP empowers fitness enthusiasts to discover partner gym facilities, schedule 2-hour workout passes, book certified personal trainers, and manage gym memberships seamlessly.

This Privacy Policy applies to all registered users ("Members", "Athletes"), fitness facility owners ("Gym Partners"), and certified trainers ("Personal Trainers") who access our platform.

We only collect data strictly necessary to authenticate your identity, book sessions, process payments, and ensure physical safety at partner gyms:

3 Payment Security & Financial Information

We treat payment information with the highest standard of security in strict compliance with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulations and global payment security standards.

Zero Storage of Sensitive Card or UPI Credentials

All monetary transactions on FITUP are processed directly through Razorpay, an RBI-licensed payment aggregator certified with PCI-DSS Level 1 (the highest global security standard). FITUP NEVER collects, handles, views, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, bank passwords, or UPI PINs on our servers.

When you make a payment, Razorpay securely transmits a cryptographically signed transaction token and payment ID (e.g., pay_xxx) to our backend. We verify each transaction server-side using SHA-256 HMAC signature validation before confirming your gym session or partner registration.

4 Third-Party Services & Subprocessors

We partner with trusted service providers to deliver our core infrastructure and services:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure cloud hosting, Firestore database storage, user authentication, and Firebase Crashlytics diagnostics. Firebase Privacy Overview →
  • Razorpay Software Private Limited: Handles standard checkout, UPI, cards, and net-banking transactions. Razorpay Privacy Policy →
  • Fast2SMS / SMS Gateway: Dispatches cryptographically generated 6-digit one-time passwords (OTP) for mobile number verification. Fast2SMS Privacy →

5 Data Retention & Security Protections

We maintain comprehensive administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your device and our servers use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Database records are encrypted at rest in Google Cloud Firestore with role-based security rules.
  • Salted Password / OTP Hashes: One-Time Passwords (OTPs) are hashed using HMAC-SHA256 with server-side secrets and expire automatically after 5 minutes.
  • Retention Policy: Active user profile data is retained as long as your account remains open. Booking history is retained for financial audit and invoicing purposes as required under applicable commercial laws.

6 Account & Data Deletion Rights

In accordance with the Google Play User Data & Account Deletion Policy, every FITUP member, gym owner, and trainer has the unconditional right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time using either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the FITUP App → Go to Profile / Settings → Click "Delete Account & Data" → Type DELETE to confirm.
  2. Direct Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address or mentioning your registered phone number to support@fitup.app or fitup.official@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Upon receipt of your deletion request, your authentication credentials, personal profile details, and active gym passes will be permanently erased from our live databases within 30 days.

7 Device Permissions

The FITUP web application and Android PWA may request the following device permissions:

  • Photo Library / File Storage (Optional): Required solely when gym partners upload facility photos or users upload gym profile avatars. We never scan or access personal galleries in the background.
  • Network State & Internet: Required to communicate with our secure backend APIs and receive real-time booking updates.
  • Push Notifications (Optional): Used to send session reminders, trainer assignment notices, and booking receipts. You can toggle notifications off anytime in your device settings.

8 Children's Privacy

FITUP does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 18 without parental consent). If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data without guardian authorization, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly purge the data.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, legal regulations, or Google Play policy updates. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page or posting a prominent notice in the application.
